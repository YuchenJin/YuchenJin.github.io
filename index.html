Biography

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Washington, advised by Arvind Krishnamurthy. I'm interested in machine-learning systems, AutoML, computer networks, and distributed systems. I received my bachelor's degree from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2015.
